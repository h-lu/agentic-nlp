## AI 小专栏 #2: 向量检索 vs 关键词检索 —— 两种范式的融合

### 搜索词: "hybrid search vector keyword RAG 2025"

**核心数据点:**

1. **Hybrid Search 定义**: Combines keyword/sparse search (BM25) and vector/semantic search (embeddings)
   - 来源: https://www.meilisearch.com/blog/hybrid-search-rag

2. **BM25 优势**: Great for exact term matching and precise queries
   - 来源: https://weaviate.io/blog/hybrid-search-explained

3. **Vector Search 优势**: Excellent for contextual understanding and meaning-based retrieval
   - 来源: https://superlinked.com/vectorhub/articles/optimizing-rag-with-hybrid-search-reranking

4. **混合检索技术**: Parallel execution, Reranking, Score fusion
   - 来源: https://scorrea92.medium.com/build-a-better-local-rag-with-hybrid-search-bm25-embeddings-10a0702dee94

5. **Azure AI Search 支持**: Microsoft 的 Azure AI Search 原生支持 hybrid search
   - 来源: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/search/hybrid-search-overview

**引用:**
- "Hybrid search combines two retrieval methods: keyword/sparse search and vector/semantic search, leveraging the strengths of both" - Meilisearch
